Dataset & Limitations

  • All 900 training examples are synthetic — authored specifically for this project. They do not represent real university syllabi.
  • Good performance on synthetic data does not guarantee equivalent performance on real-world syllabi.
  • TF-IDF cannot capture semantic equivalence across different terminology (e.g. "regression" vs. "fitting a line to data").
  • The partially_similar class involves subjective judgment and may have borderline labels.
  • Model confidence values are class probabilities from a calibrated classifier. They are NOT literal syllabus overlap percentages.
  • Topic extraction quality depends on syllabus formatting. Scanned PDFs are not supported.
